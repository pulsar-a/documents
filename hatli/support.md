# Hatli: Support

Need help with Hatli, found a message it doesn't show right, or have an idea? Write to me:

**Email: [garipov.dinar@gmail.com](mailto:garipov.dinar@gmail.com)**

I'm a single developer and read every message myself. I try to answer within a few days.

## What to include

To help me answer quickly, please mention:

- your Hatli version (⋯ ▸ **About Hatli**; on a Mac, **Hatli ▸ About Hatli** in the menu bar) and where you got it
  (Microsoft Store, Mac App Store or a download);
- your operating system and its version (for example Windows 11, macOS 15.4 or Ubuntu 24.04);
- your mail provider (for example Gmail, Outlook, iCloud or your own server), if the problem is with an account;
- if you use a screen reader, voice control or another assistive technology or setting, which one;
- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead;
- a screenshot, if it helps to show the problem. Hide anything in it you'd rather not share.

**Never send me your password**, an app password or a sign-in code, and please don't forward your own mail unless I
ask, and only if you're comfortable sharing what's in it.

If Hatli ran into an error, its log can help: Settings ▸ Storage ▸ **Open folder**, then the `logs` folder. Log files
hold warnings and errors with their technical details; look them over before you send them, and leave out anything
you'd rather not share.

## Common questions

- **Hatli can't connect to my account.** Check your password, and that your provider allows IMAP (some, such as
  Gmail, iCloud and Yahoo, ask you to turn it on or to make an *app password* when you use two-step verification).
  Hatli connects only over an encrypted connection and refuses a server whose certificate your system doesn't trust:
  it never connects unencrypted.
- **An account shows a warning in the sidebar.** It needs you to sign in again, for example after you changed your
  password or ended Hatli's access in your provider's settings. Sign in again in the account's settings.
- **Pictures in a message don't show.** That's on purpose: senders use pictures from the web to see when you open
  their mail, so Hatli blocks them until you choose. Press **Load pictures** above the message, or choose to always
  load them from that sender; Settings ▸ Privacy changes what happens by default.
- **I deleted or archived a message by mistake.** Press **Undo** in the message that appears. Afterwards, look in the
  account's Trash or Archive folder.
- **I sent a message too soon.** Press **Undo** while the countdown runs. Settings ▸ Mail ▸ **Undo send** sets how
  long you have.
- **Where is my mail kept? Can I read it offline?** A copy of your mail is kept on your computer, encrypted, so you
  can read and search it offline. Settings ▸ Storage chooses how much is kept and shows the space it takes. The
  [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md#what-hatli-keeps-on-your-computer) has the details.
- **Can I see Hatli without adding my account?** Yes: ⋯ ▸ **Open the demo mailbox** opens Hatli with made-up
  accounts and mail. Nothing in it is sent anywhere.
- **The text is too small or hard to read.** Ctrl+= makes it larger (up to 200 %). Settings ▸ Appearance has the
  high-contrast themes, fonts such as Atkinson Hyperlegible and OpenDyslexic, and a separate size for your mail's
  text. The [Accessibility](accessibility.md) page lists everything.
- **How do I connect an AI agent?** ⋯ ▸ **Connect an AI agent…** (Settings ▸ AI agents) shows what to paste into your
  agent's settings. An agent can read your mail and write drafts, as far as you allow there, but it can never send:
  you review and send every draft yourself. The [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md#ai-agents) explains what a
  connected agent can see.
- **Where are my settings? How do I start fresh?** Settings ▸ Storage ▸ **Open folder**. Uninstalling Hatli doesn't
  always remove its folders; to start fresh, quit Hatli and delete them (the
  [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md#what-hatli-keeps-on-your-computer) lists where they are). Your mail stays on your
  provider's servers.

## Purchases

Hatli has no in-app purchases, no subscription and no ads. If you bought it in the Microsoft Store or the Mac App
Store, refunds are handled by the store, not by me: request one at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) (Apple) or in your
[Microsoft account's order history](https://account.microsoft.com/billing/orders) (Microsoft).

## Security

Found a security problem? Write to [garipov.dinar@gmail.com](mailto:garipov.dinar@gmail.com) with "Hatli security" in
the subject, and please don't share it publicly before it is fixed. You'll get an answer within a week, and credit if
you want it.
