# Brinebook: Support

Need help with Brinebook, found something that doesn't work, or have an idea? Write to me:

**Email: [garipov.dinar@gmail.com](mailto:garipov.dinar@gmail.com)**

I'm a single developer and read every message myself. I try to answer within a few days.

## What to include

To help me answer quickly, please mention:

- your Brinebook version (**About** at the foot of the rail on the left; on a Mac, **Brinebook ▸ About Brinebook** in the
  menu bar);
- where you got Brinebook (Microsoft Store, Mac App Store or a download from GitHub);
- your operating system and its version (for example Windows 11, macOS 15.4 or Ubuntu 24.04);
- if you use a screen reader, voice control or another assistive technology or setting, which one;
- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead;
- a screenshot, if it helps to show the problem.

Please don't send your journal unless I ask, and only if you're comfortable sharing what's in it.

If Brinebook ran into an error, its log can help. It is in the `logs` folder beside the `journal` folder (Settings ▸
Your journal ▸ *Show in folder* shows where). Logs never hold what you wrote in your journal, but they can hold
technical details of your computer: look them over before you send them.

## Common questions

- **Where is my journal?** Settings ▸ Your journal ▸ *Show in folder*. It is one folder of plain files: copy it as it is
  to back it up.
- **How do I move my journal to another computer?** Quit Brinebook on both, copy the `journal` folder from the old
  computer's Brinebook folder into the new one's (Settings ▸ Your journal shows where), and start Brinebook.
- **I deleted something by mistake.** Press **Undo** in the message that appears at the bottom of the window. Later, it
  is still in the journal's `trash` folder for 30 days: write to me and I'll help you bring it back.
- **Brinebook says a file couldn't be read.** It was damaged or changed by another program. Brinebook moved it,
  untouched, to the journal's `unreadable` folder and opened the rest. Write to me, and I'll help you recover it; the
  journal's `backups` folder also holds each file as it was before its first change of each of the last 30 days.
- **Brinebook says the journal is read-only.** It was written by a newer Brinebook (for example on another computer).
  Update Brinebook here, and it can be changed again.
- **Brinebook says it can't save.** The disk may be full, or another program (often a sync tool) is holding a file.
  Brinebook keeps trying, and keeps what waits; when you close, it asks to try again or keeps the unsaved changes
  aside and tells you where.
- **My phone can't connect.** Your phone and computer need to be on the same Wi-Fi network. Some networks (guest,
  hotel or office Wi-Fi) keep devices apart; try your home network. If your computer has several addresses, the phone's
  pane offers another one. On Windows, allow Brinebook through the firewall for private networks if asked.
- **Is the salt amount right?** Brinebook works it out from the weights and the percentage you typed in. Check the jar
  type's empty weight and that your scale was zeroed. It is a calculation, not food-safety advice (see the
  [Terms](terms-and-conditions.md#3-not-food-safety-advice)).
- **Can I use ounces or Fahrenheit?** Yes: Settings ▸ General ▸ Measures. Your journal keeps grams and millilitres
  either way, so you can switch at any time.
- **The text is too small.** Settings ▸ Look ▸ *Text size*, from 85 to 200 %. The [Accessibility](accessibility.md)
  page lists everything else.
- **Brinebook asks something I don't want to be asked.** Tick *Don't ask again*; Settings ▸ Confirmations turns any
  question back on.
- **How do I stop the update check?** Settings ▸ Updates ▸ *Check for updates when Brinebook starts*. The store
  editions are updated by their store.
