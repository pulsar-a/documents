## 3. Not food-safety advice

Fermenting and preserving food carries real risks, including food poisoning and, rarely, botulism. Brinebook records
what you tell it and works out numbers from it; it does not know what is in your jar.

- **Salt amounts, weights and scaled recipes are calculations** from the weights, sizes and percentages you type in.
  A wrong number in (a scale not zeroed, a wrong jar weight, a typo) gives a wrong number out. Check them.
- **Stages, days and reminders** (such as how long a ferment stays on the counter) are the plan you or a recipe set,
  not a sign that a ferment is ready or safe.
- **pH and temperature lines**, including the line drawn at pH 4.6, show the readings you entered. They are only as
  good as your meter and your readings, and an acidic reading alone doesn't make food safe.
- **Recipes, ratings and notes** are yours or other people's experience, not tested instructions.
