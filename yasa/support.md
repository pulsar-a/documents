# Yasa: Support

Need help with Yasa, found a bug, or have an idea? Write to me:

**Email: [garipov.dinar@gmail.com](mailto:garipov.dinar@gmail.com)**

I'm a single developer and answer every message myself, usually within a few days.

## What to include

To help me answer quickly, please mention:

- your Yasa version (**About Yasa** in the app menu) and where you got it (Mac App Store or GitHub);
- your operating system and its version (for example macOS 15.4, Windows 11 or Ubuntu 24.04);
- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead;
- a screenshot, if it helps to show the problem.

Please don't send your `.yasa` files or passwords unless I ask, and only if you're comfortable sharing what's in them.

## Purchases and refunds

Yasa Pro is sold by Apple through the Mac App Store.

- **Restore a purchase** on a new Mac or after reinstalling: Settings → General → Yasa Pro → **Restore purchases**,
  signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
- **Refunds** are handled by Apple, not by me: request one at
  [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
