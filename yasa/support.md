# Yasa: Support

Need help with Yasa, found a bug, or have an idea? Write to me:

**Email: [garipov.dinar@gmail.com](mailto:garipov.dinar@gmail.com)**

I'm a single developer and answer every message myself, usually within a few days.

## What to include

To help me answer quickly, please mention:

- your Yasa version and where you got it (Microsoft Store, Mac App Store or Yasa's website). **About Yasa ▸ Diagnostic
  info** copies both, with your system's version and nothing private;
- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead;
- a screenshot, if it helps to show the problem.

Please don't send your `.yasa` files or passwords unless I ask, and only if you're comfortable sharing what's in them.

## Getting Yasa

- **Windows**: the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9PB134CWRR17), or from
  [yasatodo.com](https://yasatodo.com) the installer (`Yasa-win-x64-setup.exe`) or a zip to unpack and run.
- **macOS**: the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6825001497), or from [yasatodo.com](https://yasatodo.com)
  the notarized disk image (`Yasa.dmg`).
- **Linux**: from [yasatodo.com](https://yasatodo.com), a `.deb` (Debian, Ubuntu), an `.rpm` (Fedora, RHEL) or a
  `.tar.gz` for any distribution.

## Updating

- **From the stores**: the store updates Yasa. *Check for updates…* asks the Microsoft Store, or opens Yasa's page in
  the App Store.
- **From the website**: *Check for updates…* (in the menu, or Settings ▸ General ▸ *Check now*) finds a newer Yasa,
  downloads it and installs it when you quit or restart. Settings ▸ General can check at every start; that is off
  until you turn it on. On Linux, *Update* opens the release's page: install the new package as you did the first one.

## Removing Yasa

Your `.yasa` files stay where they are, and so do Yasa's settings and backups (see the
[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md), *What Yasa keeps on your computer*), unless you delete them.

- **Windows**: Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Yasa ▸ Uninstall (the Store edition and the installer's). A zip: quit Yasa and delete
  its folder.
- **macOS**: quit Yasa (its menu bar icon ▸ Quit), then move Yasa from Applications to the Bin.
- **Linux**: `sudo apt remove yasa` or `sudo dnf remove yasa`; for the `.tar.gz`, run `Yasa/install.sh --remove`.

## Purchases and refunds

Yasa Pro is sold by Apple in the Mac App Store and by Microsoft in the Microsoft Store. The editions from Yasa's
website need no purchase: everything is unlocked.

- **Restore a purchase** on a new computer or after reinstalling: Settings ▸ General ▸ Yasa Pro ▸ **Restore
  purchases**, signed in to the store with the account you bought it with.
- **Refunds** are handled by the store, not by me: request one at
  [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) (Apple) or in your
  [Microsoft account's order history](https://account.microsoft.com/billing/orders) (Microsoft).
