# AfterQuit: Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 11, 2026**

AfterQuit is an app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch that helps you quit cannabis, made by Dinar Garipov ("I", "me").
This policy explains what happens to your information when you use AfterQuit.

> **The short version: AfterQuit does not collect any data. None.**
>
> - **All your data stays on your own devices.** Your quit date, journal, notes, goals and settings are saved only on
>   your iPhone or iPad, and on your Apple Watch if you use the Watch app.
> - **Nothing is sent to me or to anyone else.** AfterQuit has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, no crash
>   reporting and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet on its own.
> - **I never see your progress, your journal or anything about you**, and I have no way to.

## Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter in AfterQuit is saved in the app's storage on your device:

- your quit date and progress;
- your journal: clean days and slips, mood, craving level, notes, and what you consumed and spent during a slip;
- your sobriety goal;
- your consumption settings (what you used to consume, how much, how often and at what price), which the app uses to
  estimate the money and time you save;
- your preferences, such as theme, language, app icon and notification settings.

Some of this, such as your journal and notes, may be personal or health-related. It never leaves your device: AfterQuit
has no account and no server, and it doesn't send your data anywhere. It is protected by your device's own security,
such as your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID.

Your device may include AfterQuit's data in its own backups, such as iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer. That
is your device's and your own choice: those backups are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy, and I receive
nothing from them.

## Widgets and Apple Watch

AfterQuit's widgets and its Apple Watch app show your progress, so the app shares some of your data with them:

- **Widgets** read your quit date and progress from storage that the app and its widgets share on your iPhone or iPad.
- **Apple Watch:** if you use the Watch app, your iPhone sends your quit date, your progress, your consumption
  estimates and your language to your paired Apple Watch, directly through iOS.

This happens only between your own devices. Nothing goes through a server, and I receive nothing.

## No analytics, no ads, no tracking

AfterQuit contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no telemetry, no usage statistics and no tracking
of any kind. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and it uses no third-party services or SDKs that collect data. It
works completely offline and doesn't connect to any server on its own.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to sell, share, transfer, request access to or delete on my side.

## Notifications

If you allow it, AfterQuit sends you reminders and notifications about milestones, streaks, goals and daily
inspiration. These are local notifications: they are scheduled by the app on your device, and nothing is sent to a
server to deliver them.

Notifications can appear on your lock screen. To keep your progress private there, turn on **Privacy mode** in
Settings → General → *Notifications*, which hides the details. You can turn each kind of notification off in the same
place, or all of them in your device's settings, at any time.

## Purchases

AfterQuit has no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no ads.

## Apple

The App Store handles downloads and updates under Apple's privacy policy. If you have chosen in your device's settings
to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and aggregated usage statistics with
me. These come from Apple, not from AfterQuit, contain nothing that identifies you, and I only use them to find and
fix problems.

## Links

The sources behind AfterQuit's science and some links in Settings open in your web browser when you tap them. What
happens on those websites is up to them and their privacy policies. If you write to me by email, I use your email
only to answer you.

## Children

AfterQuit is meant for adults, and it doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR give you rights over personal data that is processed about you. Since AfterQuit
processes no personal data on my side, I hold nothing about you to access, correct, export or erase. Your data is on
your devices, under your control:

- **Change or delete a journal entry** by opening its day in the Journal.
- **Delete everything** with Settings → General → *Reset All Data*.
- **Uninstalling AfterQuit** deletes all of its data from that device. Deleting the Watch app removes its copy from
  your Apple Watch.

## Changes

If AfterQuit ever starts handling your data differently (for example optional cloud sync or crash reports), this
policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
