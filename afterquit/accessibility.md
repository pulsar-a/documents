# AfterQuit: Accessibility

**Last updated: November 11, 2026**

AfterQuit should be usable by everyone who wants to quit. This page says honestly what AfterQuit supports today on
iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, what it doesn't support yet, and how to tell me what gets in your way.

## What AfterQuit does

- **Light and dark.** Settings → General → *Appearance*: *System* (the default) follows your device's Light or Dark
  appearance, or choose *Light* or *Dark*.
- **Your language.** AfterQuit is available in English, German, French, Spanish, Russian and Ukrainian. Change it in
  Settings → General → *Language*; the new language is used the next time the app starts.
- **Not only color.** Journal entries show a checkmark for a clean day and a warning sign for a slip, together with
  their color, and the day's sheet says *Clean Day* or *Slip* in words. Achievements show whether they are unlocked
  with icons and text, not only color.
- **Screen readers.** AfterQuit uses standard iOS controls, so VoiceOver can read its text, buttons and switches, and
  buttons that show only an icon, such as *Settings* and *Log today*, have names.
- **No time pressure.** Nothing in AfterQuit is timed or has to be done quickly. Logging a day takes a couple of taps,
  and every question can be dismissed.
- **Notifications you control.** Turn each kind on or off in Settings → General → *Notifications*, or all of them in
  your device's settings. *Privacy mode* hides their details on the lock screen.
- **At a glance.** Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and Apple Watch complications show your progress without
  opening the app.
- **Haptics** are gentle, and follow *System Haptics* in your device's Settings → Sounds & Haptics.

## Not supported yet

To be clear about what to expect:

- **Larger Text:** most of AfterQuit's text doesn't follow the system text size yet. iOS *Zoom* (Settings →
  Accessibility → Zoom) and *Display Zoom* work with it.
- **Reduce Motion:** AfterQuit doesn't follow the system setting yet. It has fade-in animations, confetti when you
  reach a milestone, and slowly moving backgrounds in the Learn stories.
- **VoiceOver** hasn't been tested end to end yet. The journal calendar, the charts and the swipeable progress
  indicators on the Home screen may be hard to use with it. A calendar day's status is told by its color; open the day
  to hear it in words.
- **Contrast:** colors haven't been fully checked against contrast guidelines yet, and AfterQuit doesn't change with
  *Increase Contrast*.
- **Orientation:** on iPhone, AfterQuit is portrait only. On iPad it works in every orientation.

Following Larger Text and Reduce Motion are next on the list. This page will be updated as they arrive.

## Tell me what doesn't work

If something in AfterQuit is hard or impossible to use for you, write to
[garipov.dinar@gmail.com](mailto:garipov.dinar@gmail.com). Please mention your device, its iOS or watchOS version,
and the assistive technology or setting you use. Every message helps decide what to fix first.
