# AfterQuit: Support

Need help with AfterQuit, found a bug, or have an idea? Write to me:

**Email: [garipov.dinar@gmail.com](mailto:garipov.dinar@gmail.com)**

I'm a single developer and answer every message myself, usually within a few days.

> **AfterQuit support can't give medical advice.** If you are struggling with withdrawal or your health, talk to a
> doctor or another qualified professional. In an emergency, or if you have thoughts of harming yourself, contact your
> local emergency number or a crisis service right away.

## What to include

To help me answer quickly, please mention:

- your AfterQuit version (shown on AfterQuit's page in the App Store);
- your device and its system version (for example iPhone 16, iOS 26.1), and your Apple Watch and watchOS version if
  the problem is on the watch;
- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead;
- a screenshot or screen recording, if it helps to show the problem.

Please don't send your journal notes or other personal details unless you're comfortable sharing them.

## Your data

AfterQuit saves your data only on your devices, so I can't see, restore or move it.

- **New device:** your data comes back only if you restore your new device from a backup (such as iCloud Backup) that
  includes AfterQuit.
- **Start over:** Settings → General → *Reset All Data* deletes everything. Uninstalling the app deletes it too. Both
  are permanent.
- **Wrong numbers?** AfterQuit counts from your quit date and your device's date and time. Check that your quit date
  (Settings → General) and your device's date, time and time zone are right.

## Widgets and Apple Watch

- **A widget shows old or empty data:** open AfterQuit once, so it can update its widgets. If that doesn't help, remove
  the widget and add it again.
- **The Apple Watch app shows no data:** open AfterQuit on your iPhone with your watch nearby and unlocked; your iPhone
  sends your progress to the watch.

## Notifications

If you don't get reminders, check that notifications are on in Settings → General → *Notifications* and for AfterQuit
in your device's Settings → Notifications. To hide what they say on your lock screen, turn on *Privacy mode* in the
app.

## Purchases

AfterQuit has no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no ads. There's nothing to buy, restore or refund.
