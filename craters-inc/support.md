# Craters Inc.: Support

Need help with Craters Inc., found a bug, or have an idea for your colony? Write to me:

**Email: [garipov.dinar@gmail.com](mailto:garipov.dinar@gmail.com)**

You can also tap **Contact support** in the game's Settings. I'm a single developer and answer every message myself,
usually within a few days.

## What to include

To help me answer quickly, please mention:

- your Craters Inc. version (Settings → *Info*);
- your device and its system version (for example iPhone 15, iOS 26.1);
- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead;
- a screenshot or screen recording, if it helps to show the problem.

## Progress and saves

Your progress is saved only on your device, so I can't see, restore or move it. It comes back on a new device only if
you restore that device from a backup (such as iCloud Backup) that includes the game. Uninstalling the game or using
*Reset progress* in Settings deletes it for good.

## Purchases

Craters Inc. is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads. There's nothing to buy, restore or refund.
